# Arm B Treatment

This treatment replaces the repository's monolithic-agent default. Use as many
native subagents as needed for the task and integrate their work before final
verification. Choose independent work packages based on the task rather than a
prescribed decomposition or worker cap.

Record `fanout_elected: true|false`, the reason, worker count, coordination time,
duplicate or abandoned work, and any semantic, interface, dependency, asset, or
path conflicts in the final response.
