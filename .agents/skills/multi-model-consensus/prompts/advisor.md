# Advisor Prompt Guidance

Ask for one independent, evidence-based recommendation. Include:

- the exact decision or unresolved question;
- constraints that materially affect the answer;
- evidence already checked;
- prior attempts and why they were insufficient;
- relevant source paths and line ranges;
- the requested output shape.

Ask the Advisor to rank alternatives, cite workspace evidence, distinguish
verified facts from assumptions, and identify the smallest verification that
would resolve any remaining uncertainty.
